## Project purpose
A US-to-Kenya arbitrage pipeline for used baby items. Source from US
marketplaces (eBay primary, Mercari/FB Marketplace/OfferUp secondary)
based on Kenyan demand signals, route through a US consolidator, ship
to Nairobi, and resell at margins that net 30%+ after all costs. The
business competes on sourcing intelligence and pricing discipline,
not on volume.

## Domain knowledge
Stack: Python 3.11+, Poetry for deps, pytest for tests, FastAPI for
any HTTP surfaces, SQLite for local state, Postgres for production.
Core libraries: httpx, pydantic, sqlmodel, structlog, typer for CLI.

Markets: eBay (US, Browse + Buy APIs), Mercari/FB/OfferUp (scraped),
Jiji.co.ke (KE primary used market), Jumia/Kilimall (KE retail
reference), Pigiame (KE price discovery), Facebook parenting groups
(KE demand signal), Instagram + TikTok (KE trend signal, weak).

Logistics: US warehouse via consolidator (MyUS or Shipito to start;
small NJ/DE 3PL at scale for sales-tax efficiency). International leg
via DHL Express, Aramex, or sea freight consolidator. KE last-mile via
Sendy, Glovo, or G4S Sendys.

Money: USD inbound (Wise Business or Mercury, virtual cards per task
for blast-radius control). KES outbound from buyers via M-Pesa Daraja
+ Pesapal for cards. FX hedge: hold KES float at Equity, convert in
batches when USD/KES moves >2% from booking rate.

Compliance: CPSC SaferProducts API for recall checks. KEBS PVoC for
KE import eligibility. Car seats have date-of-manufacture expiry
(6 years from DOM is the operating ceiling). KEBS-restricted items
list is checked per shipment. Kenyan customs: 25% duty on most baby
goods + 16% VAT + 3.5% IDF + 2% RDL on CIF value.

Margin discipline: 50% gross landed-to-retail margin is the buy-floor.
Net target after returns, damage, dead inventory: 30-35%. Items below
the floor never get bought regardless of demand score. The pricing
agent has veto power.

Inventory velocity: target sell-through under 45 days. Capital tied
up at 60% margin for 6 months is worse than 40% margin in 3 weeks.
Annualised return on inventory capital is the real KPI, not gross margin.

## Constraints — non-negotiable
- Never buy an item under active CPSC recall. Compliance Checker has veto.
- Never buy a car seat with date-of-manufacture older than 6 years.
- Never buy without the Pricing Engineer returning margin >= 50%.
- Never auto-purchase above $200 without explicit Telegram approval.
- Never push directly to main. Always branch task/<slug> + PR.
- Never store payment credentials in repo, env files, or commits.
- Never invent KE retail prices. If no source data, mark UNKNOWN and abstain.
- Never set status: approved on a buy candidate. Human gate only.
- Never bypass the margin floor with "this one is special" reasoning.

## Human gates
- Setting status: approved on any buy candidate
- Buys above $200 single-item or $500 cart total
- Merging any PR to main
- Publishing a new product category to the storefront
- Releasing held funds to a US seller after dispute
- Confirming a bulk sea-freight consolidation booking

## Skill improvement signals
After each weekly cycle, record:
- Did Pricing Engineer's predicted margin match actual sale margin?
  Drift > 10% → update landed-cost model.
- Did Compliance Checker miss a recall or DOM issue caught later?
  → update step 3 of compliance-checker.
- Did Demand Scout flag a high-score item that didn't sell in 45 days?
  → update signal weights.
- Did Sourcing Scout return zero matches for an in-demand item 2+ weeks?
  → expand source list or update query strategy.
Report these to the Curator on its next cycle.
